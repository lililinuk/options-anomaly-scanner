## Diagnostic Conclusions

1. **2026-08-21 is primarily Neighbor-driven.** All six normally eligible winners are `NEIGHBOR_DOMINATED`; none wins Volume-only, and each wins Neighbor-only.
2. **There is no evidence yet that OPEX needs more than a context label.** One date shows a smaller raw-share difference plus a much larger Neighbor difference. It cannot establish a stable historical OPEX effect or justify special scoring.
3. **OI Change Radar shows meaningful incremental discovery coverage in this sample.** Of 273 exact-joined events, 249 (91.21%) are outside the current selected Deep Dive; R2 has 63 joinable candidates and all 63 are outside it. This is coverage evidence, not a production-route decision, and the two Radar dates must remain distinguished.
4. **$150k is too low as a standalone selector, but plausible only as a permissive floor paired with another materiality condition.** It passes 319/400 (79.75%) by itself; with observed-p75 |OI Diff| it passes 86/400 (21.5%). No final threshold is recommended.
5. **R2 deserves further consideration.** It uses absolute OI materiality, represents all seven tickers, is much more selective than R1, and avoids relying on relative-change extremes dominated by tiny previous-OI bases. This is a diagnostic recommendation only.
